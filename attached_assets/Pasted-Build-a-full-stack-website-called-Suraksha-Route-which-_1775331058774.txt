Build a full stack website called Suraksha Route, which is designed to protect delivery workers and support them during emergency situations like floods, earthquakes, heavy rain, social unrest, road blockages, or curfews. The main goal of this website is to ensure that workers do not lose their income and are not forced to work in unsafe conditions.
The website will have both a frontend and a backend. The frontend will be simple, user-friendly, and easy to access so that workers can quickly check their earnings, receive emergency alerts, request compensation, and report problems. The backend will manage all important tasks such as storing worker data, tracking daily work and earnings, detecting emergency situations, calculating compensation, and sending notifications.
The system will track the daily earnings of workers and calculate their average income. If a worker is unable to work during an emergency, they will receive fair compensation based on their past earnings. The platform will also include a dynamic risk score for each zone, which will continuously analyze and mark areas as safe or unsafe. Based on this, unsafe routes will be automatically blocked to protect workers.
For safety support, the website will include a worker safety alert button that allows workers to instantly send emergency signals. It will also have an accident support workflow, which helps in reporting accidents, getting immediate assistance, and guiding the worker through necessary steps. Additionally, an insurance eligibility engine will check if the worker qualifies for insurance claims and help them receive benefits during accidents or emergencies.
Workers will also be able to raise complaints if they face unfair deductions or are forced to work in dangerous conditions. The admin panel will help manage workers, approve compensation requests, monitor emergency zones, and resolve complaints efficiently. Notifications will be sent to workers about unsafe conditions, payment updates, and important alerts.
After deployment, the system will include features like continuous monitoring, automatic data backup, error logging and recovery, performance optimization, and a user feedback and support module to ensure smooth working. Secure authentication will protect user data and maintain trust.
